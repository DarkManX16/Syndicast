Nicknames Filler Lists

A nickname is a word or a phrase in a clip’s title that stands for a show, for some seasons of a show, or for any one of several shows, like “frieza saga” for Dragon Ball Z season 3. They are taught from the “Match shows” screen of a filler list. Here you can change what one means or what it says, or delete it. Changing or deleting a nickname never changes a name already saved on a clip. It only changes what is suggested for clips whose names are not saved yet, and before you save, the clips whose suggestion would change are listed.
