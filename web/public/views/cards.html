Cards

A card is a bumper Syndicast makes itself, for a step that plays one: a few seconds of the movie it announces, with its title and when it airs over the picture, then your ending clip. Cards are made ahead of time, a week before they air, so a break never waits for one. Your real clips always come first: set the card step to play “only when” the real clip's step found nothing. A movie whose file cannot be read gets a poster card instead: its Plex art and poster with the same words.
